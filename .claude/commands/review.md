description: Weekly progress review and study plan adjustment
---

Read `/progress/daily-scores.md`, `/progress/weekly-reviews.md`, and `/progress/review-schedule.md`.

## Analyze:

1. **Score trends by domain** over the past week — improving, plateauing, or regressing
2. **Persistent weak areas** vs. improving areas
3. **Whether the current study phase is on track**
4. **Patterns in missed questions** — common error types from wrong-answer analysis
5. **Spaced repetition status** — list all concepts due for review today, overdue items first
6. **Domain weight alignment** — compare the student's study time distribution against official exam weights:

| Domain | Exam Weight | Target Study % |
|---|---|---|
| Resource and Demand Balancing | 22% (26 questions) | Should be ~22% of study time |
| Transmission | 22% (27 questions) | Should be ~22% of study time |
| Contingency Analysis and Reliability | 21% (25 questions) | Should be ~21% of study time |
| Emergency Response | 15% (18 questions) | Should be ~15% of study time |
| Emergency Preparedness | 10% (12 questions) | Should be ~10% of study time |
| Communications and Data | 10% (12 questions) | Should be ~10% of study time |

Flag any domain where study time is significantly under or over the exam weight. Weak domains should get MORE than their weight; strong domains can get less — but no domain should drop below half its exam weight.

## Then:

1. List concepts due for spaced repetition review (from review-schedule.md)
2. Adjust the study plan for next week, prioritizing:
   - Overdue spaced repetition items (highest priority)
   - Underweight domains relative to exam distribution
   - Persistent weak areas from score trends
3. Write the review to `/progress/weekly-reviews.md`
4. Recommend specific topics and commands for next week's focus
