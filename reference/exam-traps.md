# NERC RC Exam Traps

Known misconceptions and common wrong answers. The validator checks claims against these.
Add new traps as they are discovered during study.

---

## TRAP 1 — RC vs. BA vs. TOP Authority
The RC has DIRECTIVE authority over TOPs and BAs during emergencies. The BA controls generation/load balance. The TOP controls local transmission. The RC does NOT operate equipment directly — it directs others to act.

## TRAP 2 — SOL vs. IROL
ALL IROLs are SOLs, but NOT all SOLs are IROLs. An IROL specifically risks cascading, instability, or uncontrolled separation. An SOL violation may only affect a local area.

## TRAP 3 — IROL Tv
Must be ≤ 30 minutes. The RC must act BEFORE Tv expires. 30 minutes is NOT a grace period. It is the absolute maximum. Corrective action begins immediately upon identification.

## TRAP 4 — Real Power (MW) vs. Reactive Power (MVAR)
Real power → frequency. Reactive power → voltage. Students confuse which controls which.

## TRAP 5 — Frequency vs. Voltage
Frequency is SYSTEM-WIDE across the entire interconnection. Voltage is LOCAL or regional.

## TRAP 6 — COM-002 Three-Part Communication
(1) Sender issues Operating Instruction, (2) Receiver repeats back, (3) Sender confirms or corrects. MANDATORY for Operating Instructions and Reliability Directives. Does NOT apply to general information exchange.

## TRAP 7 — EEA Level Sequence
1→2→3 (increasing severity). EEA 3 = firm load interruption imminent or occurring.

## TRAP 8 — ATC Equation Direction
ATC = TTC - TRM - Existing Commitments (including CBM) + Postbacks + Counterflows. Postbacks and counterflows ADD back to available capacity.

## TRAP 9 — Reliability Directive vs. Operating Instruction
A Reliability Directive IS an Operating Instruction (subset). Not all Operating Instructions are Reliability Directives. A Reliability Directive specifically addresses an actual or expected Emergency.

## TRAP 10 — Who Calculates IROLs
The RC calculates IROLs. The TOP/BA cannot — they lack the wide-area view.

## TRAP 11 — Three Types of Transmission Limits
Thermal (current/heating), voltage (magnitude), and stability (angle/transient). The most restrictive determines the SOL.

## TRAP 12 — Next-Day vs. Real-Time
The RC must perform BOTH next-day analysis and real-time assessments (IRO-008). Not just real-time.
